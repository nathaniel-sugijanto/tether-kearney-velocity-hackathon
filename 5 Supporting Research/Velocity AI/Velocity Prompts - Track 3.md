**T3-3. How shared parts and true BOM cost are handled**

Give formulas where they exist and label them as public or your own.

**T3-4. What the linked data is used for, and how savings are found**
> Once BOMs are linked to spend, what analyses does a consulting team run to find cost savings? What match rate or spend coverage is considered good enough to start, and what benchmarks exist? Cite sources or label as an estimate.
